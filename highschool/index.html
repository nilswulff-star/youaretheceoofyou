Written by Mark HannaCommercial real estate broker, entrepreneur and TEDxMcAllen speaker. Raised by his great-grandparents, and still building.

I know what it’s like to carry more than people see.

When I walked into high school as a freshman, I was already living in my third home. My great-grandparents, born in 1912 and 1915, raised me and were my legal guardians. I didn’t have my mom or dad raising me. I lost my great-grandmother when I was 10 and my great-grandfather when I was 12.

So I paid attention. I asked adults for help. I watched people who were doing well and tried to understand how they got there. I started thinking about the family I hoped to have one day, and I wanted to make them proud.

“A student can walk into a classroom carrying a whole life nobody sees.”

That’s where this journal comes from. I want to help you see possibilities you may not have been shown yet, recognize your strengths, ask for help without feeling ashamed, and build a future that matters to you.

Eight departments. One CEO: you.

Your life works like a company with different departments. Score each one, then pick one to improve first.

Time and Habits

Plan your day. Repeat actions that help you.

Leadership

Make thoughtful choices. Take responsibility for your part.

Money

Learn to manage what you receive, spend and save.

Health and Energy

Care for your body and mind. Get rest.

Relationships

Be a good friend. Choose people who help you grow.

Reputation

Keep your word. Build trust through your actions.

Learning and Growth

Ask questions. Practice skills. Accept helpful advice.

Purpose

Know what matters to you and why you want to grow.
